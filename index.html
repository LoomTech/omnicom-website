Experience Is the Ultimate Credential

An unconventional career.

What does an aeronautical engineer, pilot in the Alaskan Arctic, international negotiator, broadcaster, and author know about communication and human performance?

Quite a lot, as it turns out.

Nila Sagadevan's career has been anything but conventional. Educated in aeronautical engineering rather than business administration, he learned the commercial ropes through international negotiations, business development, public relations, broadcasting, and the unpredictable business of dealing with people.

As founder of Omnicom Corporate Communications, he has translated that experience into executive presentations and corporate training, helping organizations communicate effectively, build trust, influence decisions, and achieve results.

Omnicom is a boutique consultancy bringing five decades of real‑world experience to communication and human performance.

A Homecoming. A Different Purpose.

Back to the land of his birth.

After a lifetime abroad, Nila has returned to the land of his birth⁠—⁠not to pursue another career, but to share lessons from an extraordinary one.

Having stepped away from corporate life, he brings home the benefit of several professional lives already lived.

His perspective was forged across diverse cultures, unfamiliar environments, demanding negotiations, and the complexities of human behavior. Today, he offers those hard-won insights to organizations and individuals ready to think differently and perform more effectively.

The experience is his. The advantage could be yours.
